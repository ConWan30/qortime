How to read these pages. Gold marks a referee-verified result only. Aqua marks a scorebug read that is not a verified result, including a final confirmed from the recording when the receipt was not signed by the QorTime referee. A FAIL tag is red, the same as in the match viewer. Controller input and haptic signals are advisory evidence, not proof: they show activity in sync with the video, not who held the controller. SELF_ATTESTED means the receipt was signed on the player's own PC. Raw controller logs are never published. These pages are separate recordings, not a ranking. No page here is referee-verified, so the scores are not a verified comparison.
