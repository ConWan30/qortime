How the final was confirmedend-menu fallback

The referee re-reads the end of the recording itself. Normally it finds a fourth-quarter scorebug in the last part of the video and checks the end-of-game menu against that read.

In this match that normal path did not apply. The referee looked at 486 frames at the end of the recording and found no fourth-quarter scorebug among them: all 486 were end-of-game menu frames. It recorded this as “no Q4 scorebug read before the end-of-game menu”.

So the referee used its end-menu fallback. It read the end-of-game menu and checked it against the last locked score in the bundle's signed timeline: away 7, home 21, Q4. They match, so the final re-read check passed. The referee's own note, word for word:

referee end-menu fallback: menu read at 0.923 over 486 frames with no Q4 scorebug read in the tail; checked against the last locked read (bundle timeline lock) AWAY 7 - HOME 21 Q4

What this means: the final comes from the end-of-game menu plus the last scorebug lock in the signed chain. It does not come from a fresh fourth-quarter scorebug read at the very end of the video.
