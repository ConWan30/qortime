Act 01 · QorCap

Capture every frame and USB pad report without touching the console.

qorcap preflight checks storage headroom, folder permissions, and wired DualSense / DualSense Edge visibility before play starts. During the run, QorCap stays lightweight — hashing video segments and chaining 1 kHz controller chunks on a single monotonic QPC clock.

  • Listen-only USB HID: never sends rumble/output/feature overrides.
  • Streaming verification: handles 600 MB/h sessions chunk-by-chunk with constant memory.
  • Game-agnostic core: works on speedruns, shooters, sims, and sports — optional game readers sit cleanly alongside.

Act 02 · QorTime

External time anchors without uploading your footage.

A local hash chain stops third parties from altering a clip afterward, but needs an external clock to show when those digests existed. Run opt-in qorcap hash-stamp against POST /aux/v1/hash-stamp to bind an Ed25519 timestamp to your session heads.

  • Zero video egress: less than 1 KB of canonical JSON crosses the wire.
  • Optional Referee Receipts: when an event uses QorTime's full referee scoring, genuine referee-verified matches can carry a VERIFIED seal — kept strictly separate from practice checks.
Act 03 · QorStream

One `.qormoment.tar` bundle. Scrubbed, offline, verifiable in 60s.

Pick any highlight or disputed moment (`±6 s`), export a self-contained uncompressed tar archive, and hand it to a speedrun moderator, league admin, or viewer. They drop it into /check in any browser — WebCrypto re-hashes every file with zero server calls.

Honest Limits (Fair-Proof, Never Overclaimed)

A passing check confirms the bytes, chained SHA-256 commitments, and QorTime timestamp match their cryptographic commitments. It does not establish:

  • Hardware operator identity: logs come from an enumerated USB pad in that session, not biometrics of who held the controller.
  • Pre-capture injection: inline passthrough adapters before the capture card or console occur upstream of the observed USB/HDMI stream.
  • Practice vs. Verified: Practice bundles stay labeled PRACTICE and use non-gold verification colors; gold is reserved strictly for referee-VERIFIED match records.
