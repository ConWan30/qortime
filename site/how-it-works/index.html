architecture · trio protocol flow

One clock.
Three verification layers.

Every claim in the Qor Trio is backed by deterministic SHA-256 chains, RFC 8785 (JCS) canonical JSON, and Ed25519 signatures — verified client-side without trusting a closed box.

01 · QorCap

Record & Hash Locally

qorcap preflight checks disk space and listen-only DualSense USB access. During play, video segments and 1 kHz controller records are SHA-256 chained (`frame-chain.jsonl`, `hand_timeline.v0.jsonl`) on one QPC clock.

02 · QorTime

Anchor Heads Without Video

qorcap hash-stamp sends only SHA-256 session heads to `/aux/v1/hash-stamp` and gets a signed `qortime.aux.hash_stamp.v1` token. Separately, optional game-specific referee flows sign full `receipt.v0` match verdicts.

03 · QorStream

Scrub, Export & /check

Export a `±6 s` `.qormoment.tar` with `hand_timeline.moment.jsonl` (`pad_id` and local paths omitted) + `hash_stamp.v1.json`. Anyone drops it into `/check` (or `tools/check_bundle.mjs`) with zero uploads.

Explicit Claim Ceiling

Passing checks use explicit status labels (`PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`, `UNMEASURED`, `ESTIMATED`). A hash stamp records when QorTime observed a set of digests — it never makes a practice capture into a gold referee verdict and never identifies who held the physical controller.
