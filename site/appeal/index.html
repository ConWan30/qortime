01 / what is in a pack

Four parts, one record.

01 · today

The receipts

The signed match receipt, the referee's status and checks, and the public log entry. Anyone can re-run it in the browser verifier.

02 · today

Key moments

Score locks from the receipt, each tied to its time in the recording and to the hashed video segments around it.

03 · today

Input-to-screen timeline

Controller reports committed in the same checkpoint chain as the video, so a reviewer can check that input, clock and screen line up. Raw controller logs are never published.

04 · coming later

Setup snapshot

A declared record of the capture chain on the capture PC, reviewed by you before it is sealed. Not in packs yet. It will never be a claim that a setup is clean.

Today a pack is a published match page with its receipt and clips, shared with the player's OK. A one-click pack from QorCap is coming later.

03 / honest limits

What a pack shows, and what it can't.

It shows

  • That controller input, game clock and screen agree, for a match recorded with QorCap.
  • That the recording was not edited after QorCap hashed it: change a segment and the chain breaks.
  • Every check, flag and limit, next to the result.

It can't

  • See inside a console or a separate gaming PC.
  • Show who was holding the controller.
  • Cover matches that were not recorded with QorCap.
  • Say that a player or a setup is clean. It is not a cheat detector.

04 / publisher appeals

No promise of an unban.

We don't promise that any publisher will review a pack, or that any ban will be reversed. Publishers decide their own appeals, and QorTime is not affiliated with any of them.

Read the publisher's own rules before you file. Activision's Appeal a Ban page says permanent bans are overturned only if the infraction was due to the account being hacked, temporary bans and limited matchmaking can't be appealed, and hardware bans aren't eligible for appeal.
