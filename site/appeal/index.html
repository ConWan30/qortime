An appeal pack is a player's own match record in one link. For a match recorded with QorCap, it shows the referee's status and every check behind it: the recording's hash chain, the controller log committed in that chain, and the timed challenges the match answered. Flags and limits sit next to the result. Bring it to a league admin, a creator, your community, or a support ticket.

01 / what is in a pack

Four parts, one record.

01 · today

The receipts

The signed match receipt, the referee's status and checks, and the public log entry. Anyone can re-run it in the browser verifier.

02 · today

Key moments

Score locks from the receipt, each tied to its time in the recording and to the hashed video segments around it.

03 · coming later

Input-to-screen timeline

Controller reports are committed in the same checkpoint chain as the video. Today a pack shows only the summary: how many reports, and that the checkpoints commit them. A per-moment input-to-screen timeline is not in packs yet. Raw controller logs are never published.

04 · coming later

Setup snapshot

A declared record of the capture chain on the capture PC, reviewed by you before it is sealed. Not in packs yet. It records what you declare; it is not a check that the setup is clean.

Today a pack is a published match page with its receipt and clips, shared with the player's OK. A one-click pack from QorCap is coming later.

03 / honest limits

What a pack shows, and what it can't.

It shows

  • The referee's status and checks for a match recorded with QorCap, including the timed challenges and how the controller log was committed.
  • That the recorded segments and controller log match the hash chain QorCap signed with the player's own capture key: change a hashed segment and the chain no longer checks. Where the referee timestamped the chain (official anchors and challenges), it also shows those parts existed by that time. It does not show the footage wasn't faked or edited before it was hashed.
  • Every check, flag and limit, next to the result.

It can't

  • See inside a console or a separate gaming PC.
  • Show who was holding the controller.
  • Cover matches that were not recorded with QorCap.
  • Say that a player didn't cheat, or that a setup is clean. QorTime does not detect cheats.

04 / publisher appeals

No promise of an unban.

We don't promise that any publisher will review a pack, or that any ban will be reversed. Publishers decide their own appeals, and QorTime is not affiliated with any of them.

Read the publisher's own rules before you file. Activision's Appeal a Ban page says permanent bans are overturned only if the infraction was due to the account being hacked, temporary bans and limited matchmaking can't be appealed, and hardware bans aren't eligible for appeal.

The same page says the account holder is responsible no matter who was playing, and that Activision won't respond to appeals sent through other channels. Our note, not Activision's: a pack is not part of that process, and as far as we know Activision has not reviewed or accepted QorTime packs.

05 / where it helps

For people who will look.

League admins

Score and screenshot disputes in community leagues and scrims, for matches that reached a final.

Creators

One link to check a clip's score moment against the signed receipt and its source segment hashes. Shared clips are re-encoded, so the check is on the receipt and segment hashes, not the clip file.

Your community

Show the record and its limits, bad games included.

Support tickets

Extra context you can attach. Whether anyone opens it is up to the publisher.
