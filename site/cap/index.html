QorCap is the lightweight Windows recorder in the Trio. It captures your HDMI feed alongside listen-only DualSense / DualSense Edge telemetry on one monotonic QPC clock — for any speedrun, shooter, sim, or competitive match.

01 · Pre-Run Gate

qorcap preflight

Checks bundle and 1 kHz hand storage headroom, folder permissions, ffmpeg readiness, and wired controller visibility (`DualSense 054c:0ce6` / `DualSense Edge 054c:0df2`) before play starts.

02 · Stream While Playing

HDMI + 1 kHz Hand Timeline

Writes 1 s SHA-256 chunks (`hand_timeline.v0.jsonl`) paired with `hand_raw.v0.bin` and segment hashes (`frame-chain.jsonl`). Strictly read-only on USB HID — zero rumble or firmware writes.

03 · Opt-In Anchor

qorcap hash-stamp

Sends only SHA-256 session heads (`hand_timeline_head`, `hand_timeline_sha256`, `frame_chain_head`, `receipt_sha256`) to QorTime `/aux/v1/hash-stamp` and writes a local verified `hash_stamp.v1.json`.

Game-Agnostic Core

Speedruns, Shooters, Sims & Teleoperation

Video frames + controller input timing do not require game-specific integration. Export any disputed moment into a scrubbed .qormoment.tar with QorStream Watch (`../watch/`) and verify offline in /check.

Optional Game Modules

Madden NFL 27 & Call of Duty Practice Readers

QorCap also ships optional game-specific modules: the Madden NFL 27 referee scorebug/challenge pipeline (required for referee-VERIFIED gold receipts) and a non-gold Call of Duty practice telemetry module.

Honest Limits

QorCap establishes a SHA-256 link between observed HDMI frames and read-only USB reports from that capture session. It does not claim hardware operator identity, protect against pre-capture adapter injection upstream of the capture card, or substitute for an external QorTime key when stamped unpinned.
