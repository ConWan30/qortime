QorCap is the one Windows app in the Capture step. It is a fair-proof of the recording and a skill-proof of the match the capture card saw: the picture, the hash chain, and the pad log beside it. It is not a name, and it is not a claim that the console obeyed the pad. When the match is over, QorTime does the rest.

  1. 01

    HDMI in

    The console picture comes in through the capture card. QorCap records that feed on the Windows PC while you play. It stays light. Nothing heavy runs during the match. The reading, the receipt, and the verdict wait until the file is finished.

  2. 02

    Segment hash chain

    As the recording is written, it is cut into short segments. Each segment is hashed, and each hash is chained to the one before it. Edit or swap a piece afterwards and the later links no longer match. That chain is the fair-proof of the file.

  3. 03

    Checkpoints

    Timestamped checkpoints commit to the chain as the match goes on. They are the marks Prove re-checks after the whistle, along with the score read off the screen. The scorebug scene on the homepage is that read. This page is the app that recorded the card.

  4. 04

    Madden NFL 27, first public path

    Capture supports a game picker. Madden NFL 27 is the first public path: the recording this site is built to hand to Prove, scorebug and all. A gold result is still a Prove outcome. It appears only when the live verifier says so.

    First public path
  5. 05

    Call of Duty, practice path

    The same picker can open Call of Duty. That path is correlated match verification, and it is a practice path. It does not carry a gold seal. A CoD HUD lock in the SAMPLE lab is correlated illustration only. It is not a seal of a Madden receipt, and it is not a public gold result for CoD.

    practice · correlated · no gold seal
  6. 06

    Controller log, since v0.3.0

    Since v0.3.0, QorCap logs controller reports from a pad that enumerated on USB in that session. The log sits with the recording. It does not prove who held the pad, or that the console obeyed it.

    usb-enumerated pad reports · since v0.3.0

Honest limits

QorTime proves the footage's integrity and timing. The Capture log is a skill-proof of what the card and the pad report showed. It does not prove:

  • Account sharing: who is physically holding the controller. The referee does not check pad identity. QorCap’s second-pad note, when it exists, stays in the local input_proof.json.
  • Right-stick injection: an inline passthrough adapter can add right-stick input the console obeys while QorCap logs the real pad. Open. Planned checks are an advisory per-axis stick-versus-camera signal first, and hardware such as a sealed tap before any pad-to-console binding.
  • Collusion: both players agreeing to throw or fake a match.
  • Anything outside what the capture card saw.
  • Red team: a virtual capture device was missed. Relays under about 2.5–3 seconds, and a splice that is rehashed and resigned, came out weak. QorCap red-team notes.
